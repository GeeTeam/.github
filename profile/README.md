# GeeTest CAPTCHA & Bot Management

GeeTest is an adaptive, AI-powered CAPTCHA and Bot Management solution that protects your website, APIs, and mobile applications from spam, credential stuffing, and automated abuse.

Trusted by over 360,000 enterprise customers worldwide, GeeTest is China's #1 CAPTCHA provider, battle-tested under ultra-high traffic pressure. It uses advanced risk engine analysis and dynamic challenge mechanisms to defend your online services against malicious bots while delivering a frictionless experience for legitimate users.
